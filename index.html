About Candis

Reporting with care, rigor, and a little persistence.

I am an independent journalist based in the Midwest, where I report long-form investigations and narrative features. My work has prompted public hearings, policy changes, and, most importantly, conversations that otherwise might not have happened.

I believe the best investigations make complicated systems legible — without losing sight of the people living inside them.
